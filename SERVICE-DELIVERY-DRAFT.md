The current evidence supports freelance video editing, agency editing projects and social-media support. ## A practical first pilot

One client, one clear audience, one manageable batch of content. A suggested four-week trial could use four short videos from client-supplied recordings. The volume and timing are hypotheses to agree with the client, not promises currently on the website.

1. Written brief: their audience, offer, desired message, existing channels, voice and goals. Ask what they want content to help achieve; do not assume every client needs maximum followers.
2. Audit the supplied profile/content. Record observations and proposed changes; describe this as an initial direction, not a proven growth system.
3. Agree one positioning draft and a few content themes with the client. Separate Ray's proposed hypotheses from facts about the business.
4. Draft concepts and simple scripts; ask the client to confirm facts, language and any professional/regulated claims before filming.
5. Supply a short filming brief. The client supplies usable footage and approves messaging. A hands-off service can reduce workload without pretending the client has no responsibility for their story or approvals.
6. Edit a small first batch in Ray's core skill area. Agree format, revisions, deadlines and approval rules before beginning.
7. Deliver approved videos and agreed supporting copy. Posting, scheduling, comment management, filming and paid advertising are optional scope items, not silently included services.
8. Review delivery and any available performance data. Distinguish output (videos delivered) from audience response (watch time, retention, saves, profile visits, genuine enquiries) and business results. Do not imply Ray caused changes without reasonable evidence.

Agree who owns footage/deliverables, what portfolio use is permitted, what is confidential, payment terms and what happens if the client supplies footage late. Do not publish client identities, logos, results or agency materials without the necessary rights.
