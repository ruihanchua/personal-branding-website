A few questions.

Do I need an audience already?

No. You can apply whether you’re starting from scratch or already posting. We can discuss where you are, what you want to build, and what support makes sense.

Is this for creators or business owners?

Creators, business owners, and working professionals can all build around their experience. Your goals and the amount of help you want matter more than your job title.

Do I have to do everything myself?

You can choose guidance while you build your skills, or explore hands-on support if you want help with execution. The scope of any work together will be agreed before you commit.

What happens after I apply?

I’ll review your answers and follow up by email to explore whether we’re a good fit. There’s no call required to apply, and applying doesn’t commit you to buying anything.
