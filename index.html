No follower count or content experience required. My content and editing work has included people starting from scratch and people with an existing audience.

Who can I work with?

Individuals starting a personal brand, freelancers, working professionals and business owners. If you want to share what you know through content, we can explore the support that fits you.

Start with your stories—the experiences, lessons and perspectives that make you, you. I’ll help you find what to share and shape it into content. For video projects, you record with guidance and I handle the edit.
