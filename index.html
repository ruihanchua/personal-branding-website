05 / BEFORE YOU TAKE THE NEXT STEP

A few questions.
A little more clarity.

Do I need an audience already?

No. You can express interest whether you’re starting from scratch or already posting. We can discuss where you are, what you want to build, and what support makes sense.

Is this for creators or business owners?

Creators, business owners, and working professionals can all build around their experience. Your goals and the amount of help you want matter more than your job title.

Do I have to do everything myself?

You can choose guidance while you build your skills, or explore hands-on support if you want help with execution. The scope of any work together will be agreed before you commit.

What happens when I join the list?

You’ll hear about coaching, classes, and hands-on support as they become available. Joining is an expression of interest. You can decide whether to take the next step when an option suits you.
