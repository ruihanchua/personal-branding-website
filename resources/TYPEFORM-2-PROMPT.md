# Paste into Typeform's AI form builder

Create a seven-screen form for Ray Chua's free content starter kit. This is a resource signup and audience-feedback form, separate from Ray's service application. Use conversational English, a beige background (#f4f1ea), dark green text (#243c32), green accents (#35654f), and a clean readable font. Do not add questions, budgets, income filters or mandatory bookings.

Welcome screen:
Title: Your stories. Your next content idea.
Description: Get 100 hook templates and a practical content-format guide. First, tell me a little about where you're starting. A few quick questions; feedback is optional. No purchase or call required.
Button: Get started

Screen 1: Where should I send your guides?
Contact information: first name and email required. No phone, address, company or last name required. Explain: I'll use your email to send the guides. If a group field cannot enforce these requirements, use the available contact layout without adding extra screens.
Include a separate optional, unticked marketing-consent checkbox in the same screen where supported: “Yes, send me occasional content tips and updates from Ray. I can unsubscribe anytime.” Not checking this must not prevent guide delivery. If the plan cannot include it within this screen, collect marketing permission separately through the email platform; keep the seven question screens and don't assume consent.

Screen 2: How did you first find me?
Single choice; required: Instagram; Friend or referral; Other; I don't remember. Allow text for Other. Add other named channels only when Ray actually uses them.

Screen 3: What do you enjoy most about my content?
Short written answer; optional.
Description: Specific posts, topics or the way I share things—anything comes to mind. New here? You can say that too.

Screen 4: Where are you with creating content?
Single choice; required: Haven't started yet; Posting occasionally; Posting consistently.

Screen 5: What would you most like content to help you achieve?
Single choice; required: Express myself and share my stories; Build my professional reputation; Grow an audience or community; Attract clients or business enquiries; Still figuring it out; Other (allow text).

Screen 6: What's making it hardest to post?
Multiple choice; allow multiple answers; required: Fear of judgement; Unsure where to start; Not enough time; Overthinking or perfectionism; Not seeing the results I want; Nothing in particular—I'm here for fresh ideas; Other (allow text).

Screen 7: What would you like to see more of on my page?
Short written answer; optional.
Description: Any topics, questions or types of content you'd love me to cover?

Before Submit, clearly link Ray's real privacy notice/contact information. Do not invent a business address or privacy URL. Do not treat form submission as newsletter consent. Submit button: Send me the guides.

Ending screen while delivery automation is NOT configured:
Title: Thanks for sharing.
Description: Your answers have been submitted. Guide delivery isn't connected yet; this form is still being set up.
Do not promise an email has been sent.

Replace the ending AFTER delivery automation has been configured and tested:
Title: You're all set.
Description: Your guides are on their way to your inbox. Check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see them. Want hands-on help putting your stories into content? You can also explore working with Ray.
Optional button: Explore working together → https://form.typeform.com/to/IFkJ6hF7
No compulsory calendar or payment.

Publish the resource form separately from the existing service application. Preserve consent status and source when connecting the email platform. This prompt creates the form; it does not set up email delivery.
