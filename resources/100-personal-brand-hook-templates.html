100 ORIGINAL STARTING POINTS

Your stories.
Your next first line.

100 hook templates for professionals, freelancers, business owners and people ready to start sharing.

You don’t need a dramatic life story. Begin with something you’ve noticed, learned, tried or changed your mind about.

Make the template yours

  1. Choose a real experience or useful idea first.
  2. Pick a first line that fits it. Replace every bracket with a specific detail.
  3. Deliver what the opening promises. Remove anything you can’t honestly support.

Original prompts by Ray Chua. These are creative starting points, not tested promises of views, clients or income. No fabricated stories, testimonials or results needed.

FROM FIRST LINE TO FIRST POST

Keep it honest. Keep it useful.

Before you record
  • Does this sound like something I would actually say?
  • Can I fulfil the opening with one clear story, explanation or demonstration?
  • Am I presenting personal experience as personal experience?
  • Have I removed private details or obtained permission to share them?

Use the companion format guide to give your idea a shape. A strong opening introduces something worth hearing; it doesn’t replace the substance.

Try a small batch. Notice which topics bring thoughtful replies and relevant conversations, not only views. Performance varies; no single hook works for everyone.
