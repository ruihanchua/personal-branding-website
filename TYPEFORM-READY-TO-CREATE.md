# Work with Ray — Typeform build sheet

Status: ready-to-create draft. No live Typeform has been created or connected.

Use one question per screen. Keep both kinds of support in one application. No budget, revenue, follower-count or compulsory phone questions.

## Welcome

**Let's talk about your content.**

Tell me a little about yourself and what you need help with. I'll use your answers to understand how I might help and follow up by email. No commitment, and no call required.

Button: **Let's begin**

## Questions

1. **What should I call you?**
   Short text · required

2. **What's the best email to reach you?**
   Email · required
   Description: I'll use this to reply about your enquiry.

3. **Tell me a little about you and what you do.**
   Long text · required
   Description: Your work, business, or something you'd like to start sharing. A sentence or two is enough.

4. **What would you like your content to help you achieve?**
   Multiple choice · allow multiple selections · required
   - Start building my personal brand
   - Help people understand my expertise
   - Attract enquiries for my business or services
   - Build an audience or community
   - Something else [enable Typeform's Other option]

5. **What do you need the most help with right now?**
   Long text · required
   Description: Maybe it's finding ideas, knowing what to say, editing, or making time to post.

6. **How would you like me to help?**
   Multiple choice · one selection · required
   - Take the lead on the content work, with my input
   - Guide me so I can learn to do it myself
   - A mix of both
   - I'm not sure yet

7. **Have a social profile or website you'd like me to see?**
   Short text · optional
   Description: Paste a link or your @handle. Just starting? You can skip this.

## Before submission

Show a privacy notice identifying the person/business collecting responses, the purpose of application follow-up, how to contact Ray about the information, and the actual retention/sharing arrangements. Supply real contact details before publication. Link the full notice if available. Do not silently subscribe applicants to a newsletter.

Submit: **Send my enquiry**

## Ending — use now, without a calendar

**Thanks — your enquiry is in.**

I'll read through your answers and reply by email. If a call would help, we can arrange one together.

Show this only after a successful real submission. No promised turnaround or automatic acceptance.

## Ending — optional later, once scheduling is connected

**Thanks — your enquiry is in.**

Want to talk it through? You can book a call below. Prefer email? You're all set — I'll follow up there.

Button: **Book an optional call** → actual scheduling URL.

If the Typeform plan doesn't support this button, use a follow-up email. Save the application before offering booking. A completed form is a lead even if the applicant never books; incomplete answers are not assumed to be captured.
