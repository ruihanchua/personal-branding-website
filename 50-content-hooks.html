THE CONTENT HOOK BANK

50 hooks.
Your ideas.
Your voice.

a free resource by ray chua

For the days you know what to say—but not how to start.

These are starting lines for your own content. Replace the brackets with your ideas and experiences. Use personal claims only when they are true for you.

ray chua · @ray.chuaa

THE CONTENT HOOK BANK / 01

Stories & personal experiences

  1. I used to think [belief]. Then [experience] changed my mind.
  2. The first time I tried [activity], I made this mistake.
  3. Nobody sees the part where [behind-the-scenes moment].
  4. I almost gave up on [goal] because of [obstacle].
  5. This is what [experience] actually looked like for me.
  6. I wish someone had told me this before I started [activity].
  7. One conversation changed how I think about [topic].
  8. The hardest part of [journey] wasn’t [expected challenge].
  9. Here’s the moment I realised [personal insight].
  10. I’m still learning [skill]. Here’s what surprised me this week.

THE CONTENT HOOK BANK / 02

Useful starting points

  1. Before you [action], read this.
  2. If you’re stuck on [problem], start here.
  3. Try this the next time [situation] happens.
  4. Here’s a simpler way to approach [task].
  5. The question I ask before I [action].
  6. Save this for the next time you need [useful outcome].
  7. If I were starting [activity] today, I’d do this first.
  8. Three things I check before I [action].
  9. You don’t need [assumed requirement] to start [activity].
  10. Your next [project] might be hiding in [overlooked place].

THE CONTENT HOOK BANK / 03

Opinions & fresh perspectives

  1. I don’t think [common advice] works for everyone.
  2. Can we talk about the downside of [popular approach]?
  3. [Common belief] sounds helpful. Here’s where I disagree.
  4. There’s a difference between [concept A] and [concept B].
  5. The advice I stopped following about [topic].
  6. Maybe [problem] isn’t about [usual explanation].
  7. I’d rather [personal priority] than [common expectation].
  8. What if you tried the opposite of [usual approach]?
  9. A popular shortcut I wouldn’t use for [task].
  10. Here’s what gets lost when we only talk about [metric].

THE CONTENT HOOK BANK / 04

Behind the scenes

  1. I tried [approach]. Here’s what actually happened.
  2. This is the part of [process] I usually don’t share.
  3. Come with me while I [specific activity].
  4. Here’s the first draft of [project]—before the polish.
  5. Two ways I approached [task]. One suited me better.
  6. What changed when I stopped [habit]?
  7. I’m testing [idea] this week. Here’s my starting point.
  8. The decision behind [detail in your work].
  9. This took more attempts than I expected.
  10. Here’s what I kept—and what I changed—in [project].

THE CONTENT HOOK BANK / 05

Conversations & relatable moments

  1. If you keep overthinking [task], this is for you.
  2. You know that feeling when [relatable moment]?
  3. A question I hear about [topic]: “[question]”
  4. What would you do if [specific situation]?
  5. Which is harder for you: [challenge A] or [challenge B]?
  6. Here’s something I’m figuring out about [topic].
  7. If you’re new to [activity], you might recognise this.
  8. What nobody told me about being a beginner at [skill].
  9. The small win that made [difficult week] feel different.
  10. I’d love to know: what changed your mind about [topic]?
