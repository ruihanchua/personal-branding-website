## What this draft is based on

Ray expects early clients mainly to want help with execution. Their confirmed experience is freelance editing since 17, content work since 18, editing across six client brands through agency projects, and social-media support for a travel agency. The copy should lead with the visitor's difficulty and the proposed support, then use that experience as evidence of the editing role.

The original script was a short introduction. This revision develops a client problem, the practical help, a scoped process, relevant experience and an application CTA. It is an original draft, not a transcription or close rewrite of another creator's video.

## Spoken draft — story-led revision, approximately 75–100 seconds

“If you want to build a personal brand, but the ideas keep staying in your notes, you’re not alone.

Knowing what you do is one thing. Working out what to say, how to film it, and how to edit it is another.

I’m Ray. I started freelance editing at 17, then began helping with content at 18. Since then, I’ve edited for six client brands through agency projects and supported social media for a travel agency.

That’s where my experience started: the practical work behind the videos.

Now I’m bringing that editing experience together with what I’ve been learning about personal branding—to help people put their own ideas out there.

Here’s how I’d approach your project. We get clear on what you want to communicate, agree the support you need, and start with a manageable batch of content. You bring your knowledge and perspective; I help with the practical work and edits.

If you want guidance, we can explore that. If you want help with execution, that’s where my editing experience comes in.

Tell me what you’re building in the short application below. After submitting, you can choose a conversation—or we can start by email.”

Confirm the proposed service scope before recording. The optional booking sentence requires a working booking option on the submitted Typeform ending screen or in follow-up email. Do not promise strategy ownership, filming, posting, growth or lead generation unless those are actually included and supported.

## How to shoot it

Use a clear face-to-camera recording, quiet audio and natural light. Film horizontally for the current landscape player. Open on the viewer's problem rather than an animated logo or long biography. A few cutaways to your own notes, planning and editing screen can illustrate the process; client materials require the necessary rights and approvals. Caption the recording, avoid tiny text, and keep the final action visible.

Upload the final recording to YouTube or a supported public Vimeo URL. In landing-page-preview.html, use Your intro video link → Connect intro video → Save my page. The opening portrait becomes the poster. The player loads only after a click. No competitor video or fake recording is supplied as the default.

## Christian’s script: analysis of the user-supplied transcript

| Beat | What the supplied transcript indicates | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name and viewer problem | Christian introduces himself and addresses social-media struggles | Establishes relevance quickly |
| Early authority | A recent-results claim appears, but the number is missing | Gives a reason to listen; the missing result cannot be reconstructed |
| Earlier uncertainty | He describes studying creators and viral content | Makes the authority feel earned and relatable |
| Specific setback | He recalls an early video reaching 356 views on its first day | Gives the struggle a concrete scene; this is his reported story, not Ray’s |
| Determined learning | Conversations with successful people, further study and paid learning | Explains the transition from confusion toward confidence |
| Solution and invitation | He presents a breakthrough and offers to help businesses through content | Bridges the story to an offer |

The useful pattern is a progression from recognition to relevant evidence, a human story, an explanation of the help and an invitation. Ray can use their real progression from editing to content support, rather than inventing a failed post, viral result or “cracked the code” moment. The transcript does not establish that the structure caused conversions.
