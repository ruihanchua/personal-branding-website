# Ray's first landing-page introduction video

Draft for Ray to edit and record. Suggested length: around 60–90 seconds, depending on delivery. This is an introduction to working with Ray, not a free class or a promise of growth results.

## Spoken draft

“If you want to build a personal brand but keep getting stuck on what to post, how to make it feel like you, or where to find the time—you’re in the right place.

I’m Ray. I started freelance editing at 17 and began helping with content at 18. My experience includes editing for six client brands through agency projects, as well as supporting social media for a travel agency.

Now I’m bringing that experience into helping people build a personal brand around what they know and care about.

We can work on getting clearer about your direction and turning your ideas into content. If you want to learn the process, there’s coaching and classes. If you’d rather have practical help with execution, we can explore that too.

Start with the short application below. Tell me where you are, what you want to build, and where you need help. After you submit, you can choose to book a call—or we can start the conversation by email.”

## Purpose of each beat

- Recognition: identify a problem the visitor already experiences.
- Introduction: name and accurately scoped editing/content experience.
- Relevance: connect that experience to the proposed help.
- Choice of support: one shared goal, with learning or execution support.
- Next action: application first, optional conversation after.

Do not say six personal-branding clients, claim to have led strategy where the role was editing, promise follower/revenue growth, or name agency clients. The draft does not use Shanghai as proof of professional results. The optional call line should only be in the final recording once a real booking option exists on the Typeform ending screen or in the follow-up email.

## Shot plan

Film horizontally (16:9), at eye level, in a quiet place with natural light and a simple background. Face-to-camera is enough. Start with the problem before introducing yourself. Add a few short cutaways only if they help: your own planning notes, your editing workspace, or personal footage. Use client footage/screens only if you have permission and it reveals no confidential information.

Use on-screen captions and check their spelling. Record the full script naturally, then edit pauses and repetition. Avoid autoplay with sound; the page uses your portrait as the poster and loads the video player only on a click.

Upload the finished video to YouTube or a supported public Vimeo URL. In landing-page-preview.html, paste its link into Your intro video link, click Connect intro video, then Save my page. The play button appears over the opening photo. No placeholder video or invented recording is published when no link is provided. Unlisted YouTube links can be used, but anyone with the link may watch them.
